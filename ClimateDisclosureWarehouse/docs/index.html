Climate Disclosure Warehouse Synthetic data

Fund climate metrics built from holdings and vendor emissions data by a SQL pipeline: financed emissions, carbon footprint, weighted average carbon intensity (WACI) and data coverage, for three made-up funds at each 31 March reporting date.

Carbon footprint

Tonnes of attributed emissions per $1m invested in covered corporate holdings.

Weighted average carbon intensity

Tonnes per $1m of company revenue, weighted by each holding's share of the covered portfolio.

Data coverage by scope

How the numbers are worked out

  1. Attribution factor = holding value ÷ company EVIC. Listed equity uses market value; corporate bonds use face value (outstanding amount), following PCAF.
  2. Financed emissions = Σ attribution factor × company emissions.
  3. Carbon footprint = financed emissions ÷ covered holding value in $m.
  4. WACI = Σ weight × (company emissions ÷ company revenue in $m), with weights equal to each holding's share of the covered value.
  5. Year matching. A 31 March report uses the financial year ending the previous 31 December. If a company has not reported it yet, the prior year is used and flagged. EVIC and revenue come from the same year as the emissions.
  6. Scope. Listed equity and corporate bonds only. Sovereign bonds and cash count in fund value but not in the metrics.
