Allocation Frontier

Monte Carlo · real assumptions

Searches 25,000 portfolios for the best chance of beating a 7.5% return objective, then stress-tests the private equity proxy that drives the answer.

Made equity-like, the maximum probability portfolio's risk rises from 8.1% to 10.8%.

Return Regimes

Regime detection · actual index data

Splits 25 years of S&P 500, MSCI World and MSCI Emerging Markets returns into regimes with a piecewise log trend, and compares indices over identical months.

S&P 500 breakpoints land on the GFC, COVID and 2022 peaks and troughs and the dot-com trough.

  • pandas
  • pytest
