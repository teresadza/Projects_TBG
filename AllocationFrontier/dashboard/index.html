How the numbers are worked out

  1. Expected return = sum of weight × CMA return. Risk = √(w′ Σ w) × √12, where Σ is the covariance of monthly historical returns.
  2. Probability of beating the hurdle = Φ((return − 7.5%) ÷ risk), the normal distribution. —
  3. Negative year, 1 in N = 1 ÷ Φ(−return ÷ risk). —
  4. Risk bands split the range from lowest to highest simulated risk into 15 equal bands, including the riskiest portfolio.
  5. Random seed 42 makes the 25,000 portfolios in each setting repeatable. Cash is capped at 5%. The three named portfolios are the current allocation and two alternatives to it.
  6. Private equity proxy. — Its returns are not smoothed (lag-1 autocorrelation about 0.1), but they move independently of listed equities, which real private equity does not. The optimiser therefore treats it as a free diversifier. The equity-like setting keeps the proxy's return and volatility and rebuilds it to move with global equities: new return = mean + volatility × (0.7 × zglobal equities + √(1 − 0.7²) × zown), where z is a standardised return.
